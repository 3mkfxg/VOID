QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Website (150 JOD) and Full Stack (225 JOD)?

The Website package (150 JOD) gives you a complete, modern website to display your business info, services, and products. The Full Stack package (225 JOD) adds a database and a private Admin Page (Dashboard) so you can log in and add, edit, or remove items and view orders anytime without needing a developer.

What does Maintenance include?

Maintenance means our team looks after your website or Full Stack system—fixing any bugs, keeping it secure and fast, performing backups, and helping you with periodic updates so you can focus on running your store.

How does the 150 JOD 6-Month Maintenance option work?

If you already got a Website or Full Stack project from us, you can add or extend 6 months of Maintenance at any time for 150 JOD so our team continues supporting and updating your project.

Will my website work well on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Most local store customers browse from their phones. Every Website and Admin Page we build is 100% mobile-responsive and fast on all screen sizes.
