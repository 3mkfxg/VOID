QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between WEB (150 JOD) and FULL STACK (225 JOD)?

The WEB package (150 JOD) gives you a complete, modern website to display your business info, services, and products. The FULL STACK package (225 JOD) adds a database and a private Admin Page (Dashboard) so you can log in and add, edit, or remove items and view orders anytime without needing a developer.

What does MAIN (Maintenance) include?

MAIN stands for Maintenance. It means our team looks after your website for 6 months—fixing any bugs, keeping it secure and fast, performing backups, and helping you with periodic updates so you can focus on running your store.

Can I start with WEB or FULL STACK and add 6 MONTH MAIN later?

Yes! You can order our 6 MONTH MAIN package for 150 JOD at any time to get 6 months of dedicated team maintenance and support.

Will my website work well on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Most local store customers browse from their phones. Every website and Admin Page we build is 100% mobile-responsive and fast on all screen sizes.
